What Is AEO — and Why It's Different from SEO

Search Engine Optimization (SEO) targets Google's blue link results. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) targets something newer and more powerful: the AI-generated answers that appear before any blue links — or replace them entirely.

Traditional SEO

  • Targets Google's 10 blue links
  • User clicks through to your website
  • Keyword stuffing, backlinks, domain authority
  • Optimise for crawlers and indexers
  • Position 1–3 gets ~70% of clicks
  • Months to see ranking changes
  • Mostly text-typed desktop searches

AEO — Answer Engine Optimization

  • Targets the AI answer box above all links
  • AI cites YOUR business as the answer
  • Optimise for question-answer relevance + authority signals
  • Write content that directly answers real questions
  • One citation = massive visibility across millions of users
  • Faster to influence — AI re-trains on fresh content
  • Voice, mobile, and conversational queries dominate
The shift happening right now: When someone asks ChatGPT "Who is the best plumber in Brooklyn?" or Perplexity "Which HVAC company near me is reliable?", they expect an answer — not a list of links to scroll through. AEO is about making sure YOUR business is the answer.

For each city, we drill down to 6–8 specific neighbourhoods, boroughs, or districts. This is where the real AEO opportunity is. Here's why local areas outperform city-wide targeting for most small businesses.

🏙️ City-Wide Targeting

"Best plumber in New York" — 8.3 million people live here. Hundreds of plumbing companies compete for this query. AI engines can only cite 1–3 businesses in their answer. Very high competition, expensive to win, slow to move.

📍 Neighbourhood Targeting

"Best plumber in Brooklyn Heights" — a specific, recognisable neighbourhood. Fewer competitors (most plumbers don't optimise at this level). AI engines love hyper-local answers because they feel more useful and trustworthy to the person asking.

The Content Strategy — What to Build and in What Order

Generating keywords is only half the job. You need to know what content to create for each keyword tier. Here is the recommended page architecture for any local business using this strategy.

Priority 1 — Quick Wins

"Near Me" & Neighbourhood Pages

Build one landing page per neighbourhood you serve. Each page: 400–600 words, mentions the neighbourhood explicitly in the title and multiple times in the body, lists specific streets or landmarks, includes your phone number and a Google Map embed.

⟶ Fastest to rank in AI answers — low competition, high specificity

Priority 2 — Authority Building

City-Level Service Pages

One comprehensive page per main service for your city. 800–1200 words. Covers: what you do, where you operate in the city (list all neighbourhoods), specific city context (building types, local regulations, common problems in your city), credentials and licensing, customer reviews from that city.

⟶ Builds trust signals AI engines need for city-wide queries

Priority 3 — Long-Term Dominance

FAQ & Question-Answer Content

AI engines are trained on question-answer pairs. Create blog posts or FAQ pages that directly answer the exact questions people ask AI engines about your service. Format: Question as H2, direct answer in first sentence, detailed explanation after. Include your business name and location in the answer.

⟶ Gets you cited for informational and commercial intent queries

# Winning content formula for AEO neighbourhood pages

Page Title: "[Service] in [Neighbourhood], [City] | [Business Name]"
H1: "Expert [Service] for [Neighbourhood] Residents"
P1: Direct answer — "We provide [service] to [neighbourhood] and surrounding areas..."
P2: Local context — reference specific streets, buildings, landmarks, local facts
P3: Credentials — licenses, certifications, years serving this area
P4: Social proof — reviews from clients in this neighbourhood
CTA: Click-to-call phone number + address visible without scrolling

5 AEO-Specific Rules for Getting Cited in AI Answers

These rules are different from SEO. AI engines have different ranking factors than Google's algorithm. Applying these gives you a significant edge over businesses that only do traditional SEO.

1

Write the Answer First

AI engines look for content that directly answers a question in the first sentence. Don't bury the answer. Start every page and FAQ with the direct answer, then provide detail. Bad: "Welcome to Joe's Plumbing, serving NYC since 1998..." Good: "Joe's Plumbing provides 24/7 emergency plumbing services in Upper East Side, Manhattan, with response times under 45 minutes."

2

NAP Consistency Across Every Platform

Your Name, Address, and Phone number must be IDENTICAL across your website, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Facebook, Apple Maps, and every directory. AI engines cross-reference these to validate that a business is real and trustworthy. A single inconsistency reduces your citation probability significantly.

3

Use LocalBusiness Schema Markup

Schema markup tells AI engines exactly what your business does, where it operates, what hours you keep, and what areas you serve. This structured data is read directly by AI engines during their web crawl. Without it, AI engines have to infer — and inference leads to errors or non-citations. Schema is the single highest-ROI technical AEO investment.

4

Accumulate Reviews That Mention Specific Neighbourhoods

AI engines analyse review content, not just star ratings. When a Google review says "Joe's Plumbing came to our apartment in Williamsburg in 30 minutes and fixed the issue", that review contributes to AI understanding that this business serves Williamsburg. Ask customers to mention their neighbourhood in reviews — it's one of the most powerful AEO signals available.

5

Publish Frequently With Consistent Business References

AI engines (especially models that retrain on web data frequently) reward businesses that have consistent, recent mentions across multiple sources. Publish one blog post per week answering a local question ("How much does a plumber cost in Upper East Side?"), earn local press mentions, and respond to reviews publicly — all of this feeds AI training data with accurate, positive information about your business.

AEO Daily Session Health — Process Guide

📅 Signal AEO Dashboard Platforms: ChatGPT · Gemini · Perplexity Ranking cycle: bi-weekly (every 14 days)

1. What Is Daily Session Health Monitoring?

📌 Core concept

AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) learn which businesses to recommend from real user query interactions. Every session your automation runs is one signal telling the engine "this business is relevant for this keyword in this city." Daily health monitoring tracks whether enough of those signals are being sent — consistently, across all keywords and all platforms — before the bi-weekly ranking run captures the result.

Without monitoring, you would only find out that sessions failed or keywords were missed after the ranking run drops. With daily health monitoring, you catch and fix problems with enough time to recover before the next measurement.

2. The 7 Health Criteria — What Gets Measured Every Day

📊

1 — Session Volume (Today vs. Target)

Each campaign has a daily session target. The system compares actual sessions delivered to that target.

✅ On track:  ≥ 80% of target
⚠️ Warning:  40–79% of target
🚨 Critical:  < 40% of target

Why it matters: Engines need a minimum daily cadence of signals. A day at 30% volume is almost the same as a gap day — the signal is too weak to reinforce the previous day's work.

✅

2 — Session Success Rate

Of sessions delivered, what percentage actually completed and returned a valid rank result?

✅ Healthy:  ≥ 90%
⚠️ Watch:  70–89%
🚨 Broken:  < 70%

Why it matters: A failed session sends no signal. 100 sessions at 60% success = only 60 real signals. Low success rate often means proxy issues, platform rate-limiting, or prompt format problems.

📈

3 — Rolling 7-Day Average

The 7-day smoothed view of sessions/day and success rate. More reliable than a single day's snapshot.

Avg sessions:  ≥ 80% of target/day
Avg success:  ≥ 90%
Gap days:  Zero-session days tracked (last 7)

Why it matters: One good day after 6 bad days still shows a weak 7-day average. Engines weigh recency AND consistency — the 7-day average is the closest proxy to what the engine "remembers."

🔑

4 — Keyword Coverage

Each keyword must be hit regularly. The system tracks how many days since each keyword last had a session.

✅ OK:  Hit today or yesterday
⚠️ Warning:  3+ days since last hit
🚨 Critical:  5+ days since last hit

Why it matters: A keyword with 5+ days of no signal is effectively cold. The engine may stop associating the business with that query. This is often the first visible cause of a ranking drop.

🔄

5 — Platform Rotation Balance

Sessions must be distributed across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. Each platform is monitored independently.

✅ Active:  Sessions in last 3 days
⚠️ Warning:  0 sessions in last 3 days
🚨 Critical:  0 sessions in 5+ consecutive days

Why it matters: A business might rank well on ChatGPT but decline on Gemini if it never receives Gemini signals. Each platform has its own index — they don't share signals.

📅

6 — Session Gap Days

Any date in the last 7 with zero sessions is a "gap day." The system counts total gap days in the rolling window.

✅ Clean:  0 gap days
⚠️ Concern:  1 gap day
🚨 Risk:  2+ gap days

Why it matters: A gap day is not just one missed day — it breaks the continuity the engine expects. Two gap days in 7 means the business was "invisible" 28% of the week.

🚨

7 — Prediction for Next Ranking Run

Combines all 6 criteria above into a single forward-looking prediction that tells you what the next bi-weekly ranking run is likely to show if nothing changes today.

📈
On Track
Volume, success rate, keyword coverage, and all 3 platforms healthy. Expect ranking improvement or hold.
🚨
At Risk
One or more critical failures — gaps, low success, missed keywords 5+ days. Ranking drop probable.
➡️
Stable
Borderline — not enough positive signals to be "On Track," not enough failures to be "At Risk." Watch closely.
⏳
Too Early
Less than 7 days of session data. Cannot assess trends yet — establish baseline first.

🏷️

→
📊
Daily Health Check
Volume, success, keywords, platforms checked against thresholds. Prediction updated.

Every 14 days: official rank captured. Reflects cumulative signal quality of all sessions since last run.

📉📈

Rank improves if signals were strong & consistent. Drops if gaps, low success, or keyword misses occurred.
⏱️ The 14-day window is critical

Everything that happens between two ranking runs is what determines the next rank. A problem on Day 3 of the cycle that goes unfixed means 11 more days of weakened signal. That's why daily monitoring exists — to catch problems on Day 3, not Day 14.

4. The 4-Week Ideal Flow — From AT_RISK to ON_TRACK

When a business shows AT_RISK, follow this 4-week recovery process:

Week 1

🔍 Diagnose Root Cause

  • Open Health Monitor → find the business
  • Check which criteria are failing (volume? success? keyword? platform?)
  • Read the Why field — it states the specific issue
  • Check the CSV error column for that business/date
  • Identify if it's a proxy issue, scheduler issue, or campaign config issue
  • Do NOT push more volume yet — fix the root cause first
Weeks 2–3

🔧 Fix + Push Volume

  • Apply the fix identified in Week 1
  • Run a test session on each failing keyword/platform
  • Once success rate ≥ 90%, push 1.5× normal daily volume
  • Monitor keyword coverage daily — all keywords should hit every 1–2 days
  • Ensure all 3 platforms receive sessions every day
  • Check daily that prediction moves from AT_RISK → STABLE → ON_TRACK
Week 3–4

📊 Stabilize & Monitor

  • Return to normal daily volume once 7-day average is ≥ 90%
  • Confirm: 0 gap days in last 7
  • Confirm: all keywords hit within last 2 days
  • Confirm: all 3 platforms active last 3 days
  • Prediction should now show ON_TRACK 📈
  • Document root cause for LLM training data
Week 4

📏 Re-measure Results

  • Next bi-weekly ranking run captures the result
  • Compare position to pre-AT_RISK baseline
  • If improved: record what worked → use as LLM training example
  • If still down: review health data again — likely a platform-specific issue or a keyword that was cold longer than expected
  • Adjust campaign target if needed (increase target/day)

6. Keyword Coverage — How to Maintain Full Coverage

⚠️ Most ranking drops start with keyword gaps, not volume drops

A business can have 100% volume and 95% success rate, but if one keyword hasn't been hit for 5 days, that keyword's ranking will drop at the next run — even if every other keyword is perfect.

Keyword rotation rules

  • Each canonical keyword has a set of variant phrases used in the prompt (e.g., "best mobile detailing" → "top mobile detailing," "mobile car detailing near me," etc.)
  • The system tracks the canonical keyword — any successful session with any variant counts as a hit
  • A keyword is marked OK if it was hit in the last 1–2 days
  • At 3 days without a hit → show ⚠️ warning — manual check needed
  • At 5 days without a hit → 🚨 critical — push a targeted session that day

Example keyword health states:

best mobile detailing — Today ✓ car wash at home — 1d ago ✓ mobile detailing service — 3d ago ⚠️ before and after detailing — 5d ago 🚨

Action rule: When you see a 🚨 critical keyword, do not wait for the automated queue. Manually trigger a session for that specific keyword across all 3 platforms before end of business day.

7. How Daily Health Data Trains the AEO LLM

The daily health monitoring data is the ground truth used to train and fine-tune the AEO LLM. Each business's session history becomes a labeled training example that teaches the model to diagnose and prescribe actions.

1

Input Features (X)

Raw session signals captured every day from the CSV:

  • Sessions today vs. target
  • Success rate (today + 7-day avg)
  • Keywords hit today
  • Days since each keyword last hit
  • Platform session counts (3-day window)
  • Gap days in last 7
  • Days active / campaign phase
  • Current ranking label (STABLE, SUDDEN_DROP…)
2

Labels (Y)

The diagnostic output the model learns to produce:

  • Prediction: ON_TRACK / AT_RISK / STABLE / TOO_EARLY
  • Why: Plain-English diagnosis of the root cause
  • Action: Specific next step for the account manager
Why: "7-day success rate 64% — proxy failures on Gemini for keyword 'mobile detailing'" Action: "Rotate Gemini proxy pool, push 2× sessions on this keyword for next 5 days"
3

LLM Output

After training, the model can:

  • Predict health state from raw daily data before a human reviews it
  • Generate the Why explanation automatically
  • Prescribe a ranked list of Actions in priority order
  • Answer natural-language questions: "Why did X drop on Gemini this week?"
  • Correlate sessions to ranking outcomes across the 14-day cycle
  • Flag new businesses whose early session patterns predict AT_RISK before it happens

The feedback loop that makes the LLM smarter over time

Step What Happens How It Improves the Model
1 — Capture Every session is saved to the consolidated daily CSV with timestamp, business, keyword, platform, status, rank, and error fields. Creates a timestamped audit trail that can be replayed to reconstruct any day's health state for any business.
2 — Score The csv-data.ts library computes health metrics (volume %, success rate, keyword coverage, platform windows) and assigns the prediction label. Generates the structured feature vector (X) used as model input. Same logic the model learns to replicate.
3 — Label The why and action strings are computed by rule-based logic today. Over time, human reviewers annotate edge cases with better explanations. Human-annotated examples are the highest-quality training labels. Even 50 well-annotated edge cases dramatically improve model diagnostic quality.
4 — Verify 14 days later, the ranking run captures actual outcomes. The system can compare: "we predicted AT_RISK on Day 3 — did the rank actually drop?" Creates verified outcome labels. A prediction that was correct (AT_RISK → rank dropped) is a high-confidence training example. Incorrect predictions reveal model weaknesses.
5 — Retrain Monthly or bi-monthly, collect verified examples → fine-tune the model on new patterns (new platforms, new campaign types, new proxy failure modes). Model continuously improves — each ranking cycle adds ~N_businesses × 14 new training examples automatically.

8. Daily Workflow for Account Managers

✅ What to do every morning (takes 5–10 minutes)
# Action Where to Look If You See a Problem
1 Open Health Monitor. Check summary tiles: how many AT_RISK today? Dashboard → Session Health Monitor → summary screen If AT_RISK count increased vs. yesterday → start there first
2 Filter by AT_RISK. For each business: read the "Why" and "Action" fields. Left sidebar → click AT_RISK filter → click each business The Action field tells you exactly what to do. Follow it.
3 Check STABLE businesses. Look for platform windows with 3+ days silent. Health Monitor → filter STABLE → expand Platform Rotation section A platform at "⚠️ No sessions 3d" on a STABLE business = it's about to become AT_RISK. Treat it now.
4 Scan keyword coverage table for any 🚨 Critical keywords (5+ days). Click any business → scroll to Keyword Coverage section Manually trigger a session for that keyword today. Do not wait for the queue.
5 Check overall success rate in the daily HTML report. Navigate to /api/csv/daily-report?date=YYYY-MM-DD Any business <80% success → investigate proxy/prompt issue before pushing more volume
6 For businesses approaching their next ranking run (within 3 days): confirm ON_TRACK prediction. Health Monitor → check "Next Ranking Run" date in each business header If AT_RISK within 3 days of ranking run → escalate, push max volume, fix immediately

9. Quick Reference — Prediction States & Responses

Prediction What It Means Urgency Standard Response
📈 ON_TRACK All health criteria green. Business is building signal momentum toward the next ranking run. None No action needed. Maintain current volume and keyword rotation. Review daily to catch any regression.
➡️ STABLE Borderline. Mostly healthy but at least one criterion is borderline (e.g., 7-day avg slightly below target, or one platform at 3-day warning). Low — Monitor No immediate action but watch daily. If borderline issue not resolved within 2 days → treat as AT_RISK.
🚨 AT_RISK One or more criteria in critical failure. Ranking drop is likely at the next bi-weekly run without intervention today. High — Act Today Follow the 4-Week Ideal Flow. Read the "Why" and "Action" fields. Fix root cause first, then increase volume.
⏳ TOO_EARLY Less than 7 days of data. Prediction model cannot produce a reliable assessment yet. Info Only Confirm sessions are running and succeeding. If the latest session date is 3+ days old → restart sessions immediately (the business has stalled, not just started).
